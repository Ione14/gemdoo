Privacy Policy

Gemdoo - Gemstone Sorting Puzzle Game

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Gemdoo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use our mobile puzzle game, contact support, or interact with advertising services.

2. Game Progress and Preferences

Gemdoo is designed to store gameplay progress, completed levels, and preferences such as sound and vibration settings locally on your device.

This information is used to maintain your game experience. Removing the app or clearing its storage may delete locally saved progress.

3. Advertising

Gemdoo is intended to be supported by advertising provided through Google AdMob. Advertisements may include banners, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads used to obtain hints.

Depending on the device, region, advertising configuration, and applicable consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, and diagnostic or performance data.

Such information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's policies.

For additional information, visit Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law, Gemdoo will request advertising-related consent through a consent management mechanism such as Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Users may be offered privacy choices according to their region and applicable regulations. Refusing personalized advertising consent does not automatically prevent access to the game.

5. Contact and Support

If you contact us by email or through a support form, we may process the name, email address, message, and other information you voluntarily provide.

This information is used to respond to your inquiry and provide support. It is retained only as reasonably necessary for these purposes or applicable legal obligations.

6. Third-Party Services

Gemdoo may use third-party services for advertising and support functionality. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

Before each production release, the services and disclosures must reflect the actual integrations used by the application.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with Gemdoo. However, no electronic communication or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to device behavior.

For questions about personal information provided through support, including requests for access, correction, or deletion, please contact us using the address below.

Information processed by third-party providers is also subject to their applicable retention and deletion policies.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent where applicable.

Requests can be submitted using the contact information below.

10. Children's Privacy

Gemdoo's privacy and advertising practices must comply with applicable age-related requirements and platform policies. Advertising and consent handling may depend on the age group for which the app is made available.

11. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as Gemdoo develops or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
